Somjit Nath

I am a PhD Student at at Mila/ÉTS where I am advised by Professor Samira Ebrahimi Kahou. I am currently working on representation learning. Previously, I was a Researcher at TCS Research, working under the supervision of Harshad Khadilkar, where I worked on applying Reinforcement Learning to Supply Chains and addressing the challenges that come with it.

I did my Masters from University of Alberta where I was advised by Martha White. My thesis formulates a new approach for training Recurrent Neural Networks. I also worked on non linear value function approximation for Reinforcement Learning. My undergrad was in Electrical Engineering from Jadavpur University, during which I worked mostly in the Internet of Things (IOT) domain along with medical imaging.

Research

My main interests lie in the field of reinforcement learning. My fascination for RL developed after taking Rich Sutton's course during my Masters. In the context of RL, I am also interested in representation learning, model based RL and exploration.
